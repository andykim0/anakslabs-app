Our Team

Every surgeon at Northbank is fellowship trained, and every therapist on our staff works only with orthopedic and sports medicine patients. We keep the team small on purpose: you should be able to reach the person who treated you.

Dr. Alina Reyes, MD

Dr. Alina Reyes, MD is a board-certified orthopedic surgeon who completed her sports medicine fellowship in Chicago. She treats knee and shoulder injuries and serves as a team physician for two local high schools. She has practiced in Chicago for fourteen years and speaks English and Spanish.

Dr. Marcus Whitfield, MD

Dr. Marcus Whitfield, MD is a board-certified orthopedic surgeon focused on joint replacement of the hip and knee. He completed his residency and adult reconstruction fellowship in the Midwest and has performed joint replacement surgery for eighteen years. He sees patients at both clinics.

Physical therapy team

Our therapists are licensed doctors of physical therapy and see patients in the same building as the surgical team. Coordinating both under one roof shortens the gap between an operation and the first rehabilitation session, which is the part of recovery patients most often ask about.
