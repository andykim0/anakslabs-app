Orthopedic and sports medicine care in Chicago

Northbank Orthopedic & Sports Medicine treats injuries and joint conditions for adults and young athletes across Chicago and the western suburbs. Our surgeons and physical therapists work in the same building, so the person who plans your surgery is the person who follows your recovery.

What we treat

  • Knee Replacement
  • ACL Reconstruction
  • Rotator Cuff Repair
  • Sports Injury Care
  • Joint Injections
  • Hand and Wrist Surgery

We also publish plain-language explanations of the two areas patients ask about most: sports medicine and joint replacement.

Our team has cared for the region's athletes and working adults for over twenty years of experience in orthopedic practice. We accept most major insurance plans and offer early morning appointments for patients who cannot miss a work day.
