Sports Injury Care

We see athletes at every level, from professionals to adults who play once a week. Most sports injuries are not surgical: sprains, muscle strains, tendon overload and stress reactions make up the majority of what walks through the door, and they respond to accurate diagnosis and a graded loading programme rather than an operation.

What the procedure involves

Same-week appointments are held open for acute injuries during the season. If you are injured on a Saturday, call on Monday and you will be seen that week rather than in a month. Imaging is arranged in the same building when it changes the plan and skipped when it does not.

Who it suits

For young athletes we look at training load as well as the injured part. A stress injury in a fifteen year old who has just moved from one sport to three is a workload problem wearing the costume of an ankle problem, and treating only the ankle sends them back to the same place.

Recovery and follow-up

Our therapists build return-to-play programmes with the athlete's coach where the athlete wants us to. Clearing someone to train is a different decision from clearing them to compete, and we make both explicitly rather than leaving it to be inferred.

Individual results vary, and every procedure on this page carries risks and limitations that we discuss with you before you decide.
