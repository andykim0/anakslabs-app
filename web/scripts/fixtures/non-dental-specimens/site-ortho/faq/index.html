Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a referral to be seen?

Most plans allow you to book directly with an orthopedic specialist. A few require a referral from your primary care physician first. Our staff check when you call so you are not turned away at the desk.

How long will I wait for an appointment?

Routine appointments are usually within two weeks. Acute sports injuries are seen within the same week during the season, and we hold slots open for that purpose.

Will I need surgery?

Most patients we see do not. Injections, therapy and time resolve the majority of orthopedic problems. We will tell you plainly when an operation is the better option and when it is not.

Can I see the same surgeon at both clinics?

Yes. Our surgeons see patients at both the River North and Oak Park clinics, and you can choose whichever is easier to reach.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Bring your insurance card, any imaging you have already had, a list of your medications, and shorts if the problem is in your knee or hip.
