Sports Medicine

Sports medicine is the part of orthopedics concerned with keeping active people active. This page explains how we think about training load, why most injuries in recreational athletes are overuse rather than accident, and what a graded return to play actually involves week by week.

An overuse injury is a mismatch between what tissue can tolerate and what it is being asked to do. The tissue is rarely abnormal; the load curve is. That is why the first question we ask about a sore Achilles is not what it feels like but what changed in the six weeks before it started hurting.

Graded return to play means adding one variable at a time: volume, then intensity, then change of direction, then contact. Adding two at once is how an athlete who felt ready ends up back at the start. We write the progression down so the athlete and the coach are reading the same plan.
