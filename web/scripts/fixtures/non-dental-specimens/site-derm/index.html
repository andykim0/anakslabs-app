Medical and surgical dermatology in Portland

Larkfield Dermatology treats skin, hair and nail conditions for adults and children, and removes skin cancers. We are a single-location practice, which means the dermatologist who diagnoses you is the one who treats you and the one who sees you at follow-up.

What we treat

  • Skin Cancer Screening
  • Mohs Surgery
  • Acne Treatment
  • Eczema and Psoriasis
  • Laser Resurfacing
  • Cosmetic Injectables

Our laser resurfacing programme is designed for immediate improvement in skin texture, and we review the plan with you at every visit. Individual results vary, and every treatment on this page carries risks and limitations that we discuss with you before you decide.

We reserve same-week appointments for changing or bleeding lesions. If something on your skin has changed in the last month, say so when you call and we will fit you in.
