Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I be seen for a changing mole?

Within the same week. Tell the receptionist that a lesion has changed and you will be triaged into an urgent slot rather than a routine one.

Do you see children?

Yes. Dr. Nakamura runs a paediatric dermatology clinic on Thursday mornings with longer appointment slots for eczema and birthmark visits.

Will my biopsy leave a scar?

Any procedure that removes skin leaves a mark. We choose the technique and the placement to make it as inconspicuous as we can, and we tell you what to expect before you consent.

How long do results take?

Pathology results take about a week. We telephone you with them whatever they show, and we do not leave diagnoses on voicemail.

Do you offer cosmetic treatment on the same visit?

Not at the first appointment. Cosmetic treatments begin with a consultation so that you have time to consider the plan and the cost before anything is done.
