General Dermatology

This page explains what a general dermatology appointment covers and how to prepare for one. It is the appointment to book when something on your skin is bothering you and you are not sure which of our clinics it belongs in.

Bring a list of what you have already tried, including things bought without a prescription. Half of the useful information in a first dermatology appointment is the history of what has and has not worked, and it is very hard to reconstruct that from memory in the room.

If the problem is intermittent, photograph it when it is present. A rash that has settled by the time you reach us is much easier to diagnose from your photograph than from a description.
