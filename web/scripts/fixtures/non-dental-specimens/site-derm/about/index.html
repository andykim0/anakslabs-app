Our Team

Larkfield is a two-dermatologist practice supported by a nurse practitioner and a small clinical team. We deliberately kept the practice small so that continuity is real rather than aspirational.

Dr. Priya Raman, MD

Dr. Priya Raman, MD is a board-certified dermatologist and fellowship-trained Mohs surgeon. She completed her dermatology residency on the west coast and has practised in Portland for eleven years. Her clinical focus is skin cancer diagnosis, Mohs surgery and complex reconstruction.

Dr. Ellis Nakamura, MD

Dr. Ellis Nakamura, MD is a board-certified dermatologist who treats inflammatory skin disease in adults and children, including eczema, psoriasis and severe acne. He runs our paediatric clinic on Thursday mornings and reads our practice's pathology alongside the laboratory.

Clinical team

Our nurse practitioner sees follow-up visits for acne, eczema and psoriasis, and our medical assistants have all worked in dermatology for more than five years. You will usually see the same two or three faces every time you come in, which matters when a condition is being tracked over years rather than weeks.
