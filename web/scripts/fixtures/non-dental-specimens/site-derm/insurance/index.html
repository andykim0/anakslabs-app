Insurance and Payment

Larkfield accepts most major insurance plans, including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare, Providence, Moda and Medicare. Medical dermatology visits, skin cancer screening and surgery are covered by most plans.

Cosmetic treatments, including injectables and laser resurfacing for cosmetic indications, are not covered by insurance. Prices are published on request and quoted before treatment. We do not offer package deals or discounts tied to booking on the day.

We verify your benefits before the visit and tell you your expected cost in advance. If a biopsy is taken, the pathology is billed separately by the laboratory, and we will say so at the time rather than letting a second bill surprise you.
