Acne Treatment

Acne is a medical condition with effective treatments, not a phase to be waited out. We treat it in teenagers and in adults, and we take adult acne seriously because it behaves differently and responds to different medication than the teenage pattern.

What the procedure involves

Treatment is matched to the type of acne rather than its severity alone. Comedonal acne, inflammatory acne and nodular acne need different approaches, and hormonal patterns in adult women often respond to treatments that do nothing for a fifteen year old with the same score on paper.

Who it suits

Most treatments take eight to twelve weeks to show their full effect, which is the single most common reason people abandon a plan that was working. We book the review at the eight week mark when we start, so the check-in is already in the diary.

Recovery and follow-up

For severe or scarring acne we discuss isotretinoin. It is a highly effective medication with monitoring requirements and important precautions, all of which we go through in detail before anyone starts it.

Individual results vary, and every treatment on this page carries risks and limitations that we discuss with you before you decide.
