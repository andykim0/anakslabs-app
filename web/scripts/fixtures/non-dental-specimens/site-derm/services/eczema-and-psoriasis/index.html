Eczema and Psoriasis

Eczema and psoriasis are long-term inflammatory conditions. Neither is cured by a single prescription, and both are far more controllable than most people arriving at our clinic have been led to believe. The goal we set with you is clear skin most of the time, not a temporary improvement.

What the procedure involves

For eczema, treatment starts with getting the barrier working: the right emollient used often enough, topical anti-inflammatories used correctly during flares, and identifying the triggers that matter for you specifically rather than the generic list.

Who it suits

For moderate to severe disease that topical treatment cannot hold, we use phototherapy, systemic medication and biologics. Biologic therapy has changed what is achievable in psoriasis in particular, and we handle the insurance authorisation for it in-house.

Recovery and follow-up

Children with eczema are seen in our Thursday clinic with longer appointment slots, because teaching a family how and when to apply treatment takes more than ten minutes and is the part that determines whether it works.

Individual results vary, and every treatment on this page carries risks and limitations that we discuss with you before you decide.
