Laser Resurfacing

Laser resurfacing removes damaged outer layers of skin in a controlled way so that smoother skin grows in their place. We use it for sun damage, fine lines, uneven pigment and some scarring.

What the procedure involves

We use both ablative and non-ablative lasers and the choice is a trade-off. Ablative resurfacing achieves more in one session and requires a week or more of visible healing. Non-ablative treatment requires a course of sessions and lets you return to work the next day.

Who it suits

Preparation matters more than people expect. Skin is prepared for several weeks beforehand, sun exposure has to be avoided before and after, and patients with a history of cold sores are given antiviral medication in advance.

Recovery and follow-up

Healing follows a predictable course: redness and swelling for the first few days, peeling through the first week, and a pink phase that fades over several weeks. Sun protection afterwards is not optional; it determines whether the result holds.

Individual results vary, and every treatment on this page carries risks and limitations that we discuss with you before you decide.
