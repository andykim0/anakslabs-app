Cosmetic Injectables

Cosmetic injectable treatments are performed by our dermatologists rather than delegated. That is a deliberate choice: the anatomy that makes these treatments safe is the same anatomy we work with in surgical dermatology.

What the procedure involves

Botulinum toxin softens the lines produced by muscle movement, principally across the forehead and around the eyes. The effect develops over about two weeks and lasts three to four months. Dermal fillers restore volume where fat has been lost and are used along the cheeks, temples and jawline.

Who it suits

The first appointment is a consultation, not a treatment. We look at your face at rest and moving, explain which of your concerns these treatments can and cannot address, and quote you before anything is booked. Some people leave that appointment having decided not to proceed, which is a perfectly good outcome.

Recovery and follow-up

We review every patient two weeks after their first treatment, at no charge, to assess the effect and adjust the plan for next time.

Individual results vary, and every treatment on this page carries risks and limitations that we discuss with you before you decide.
